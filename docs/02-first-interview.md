# First Interview

## Goal

Prepare the first interview with the operator, to understand the current paper process and how the invoice is prepared, and to start testing the hypotheses in [01-initial-hypotheses.md](01-initial-hypotheses.md).

## Questions

### Volume

- How many requests per month?
Totals are computed once a month, 300-500 entries per month

- How many different staff members?
Dozens of staff members, including internal university staff and teachers that come and go. In total, there are 12 different departments.

### Paper Record

- What exactly is written on the paper: name, department, pages, color or black-and-white, date?
In spanish: Fecha, area, nombre, descripción, copias, anillados, firma
- What goes wrong today: illegible entries, missing entries, disputes with the University? (see H2)
Making the report takes time and there are many mistakes: people register their entry in the wrong department, sometimes entries are unreadable

### Invoice

- How often does she bill the University? Is it monthly?
Monthly, at a specific date (24 th)
- How does she handle the invoice today, step by step?
She takes a photocopy of the paper trail, prepares an excel report and takes it to the University Administrator. The University Administrator then takes that report to every department manager for a signature.
- How long does it take her to prepare each invoice?
It could be done in one hour per month, but she never manages to do it without interruptions, so it takes one to two days
- Does the invoice show money amounts (unit prices × quantities), or only quantities such as pages and copies?
Only quantities of copies.

- Do prices depend on anything besides color, such as paper size (carta, oficio) or single- vs double-sided printing?
She makes a conversion (e.g. a color copy is worth 5 simple copies). Even more complex services are converted to copies, and she wants to keep it this way.

- What does the University ask for along with the invoice: a list of requests, totals per department, or totals only? (see H1)
Yes, they see the whole paper

- Has an invoice ever been questioned or rejected? What happened? (see H1, H2)
Nothing relevant for this project,.

### University

- Who at the University receives and approves the invoice? Do they ever look at the paper record?
The University Administrator and her assistant

- Would the University mind if an app is used? Privacy of staff names, etc.
She want to ask once we have an MVP, it could be rejected. Currently each entry has a signature, with an app there wouldn't be signatures, this is a risk.

### Operator

- Does she have a phone or computer she would be willing to use for the tool?
She is willing to check if she can buy a tablet for this. This would be necessary because the internet is extremely unreliable at that location, both wifi and data.
